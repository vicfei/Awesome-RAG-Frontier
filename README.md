## Why another RAG list?

Most "Awesome RAG" lists are paper catalogs that drift stale — a 30k-star RAG platform can ship a major release on a Tuesday and still be missing from all of them a year later. This tracker bets on three things they don't:

1. **Engineering + research, equally.** Products, frameworks, parsing, evaluation, and GraphRAG get first-class rows next to papers — because the frontier now ships as repos, not just PDFs.
2. **Freshness enforced by machines.** Star counts, activity dates, and a weekly arXiv digest are regenerated by a [GitHub Action](.github/workflows/refresh.yml) every Monday. Stale tables are a bug, not a lifestyle.
3. **Both ecosystems.** The English and Chinese RAG communities build on each other but rarely read each other. Every entry carries a bilingual one-liner.

Inclusion is rule-based, not vibes-based: see **[criteria.md](criteria.md)**.

### Paradigm papers (the vocabulary people still use)

| Paper | Year | The idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [HyDE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10496) | 2022 | Embed a hypothetical answer instead of the query. |
| [FLARE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06983) | 2023 | Retrieve actively, mid-generation, on low-confidence tokens. |
| [Self-RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11511) | 2023 | The model critiques its own retrieval and citations. |
| [CRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15884) | 2024 | A corrector that salvages bad retrieval before generation. |
| [RAPTOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18059) | 2024 | Recursive tree summarization as the index. |
| [HippoRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831) | 2024 | Knowledge graph + personalized PageRank, hippocampus-inspired. |
| [LightRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05779) | 2024 | Graph + vector dual-level retrieval, EMNLP 2025. |
