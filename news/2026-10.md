# This Month in RAG — 2026-10

## 2026-10 — The decision-layer wave

A class of **decision-only models** — no text generation, just typed, calibrated decisions — erupted in late September and is being spliced into RAG pipelines between retrieval and generation: rerank, filter, route, verify. The proprietary spark is [Jev](https://typesafe.ai/) (TypeSafe; three primitives: Noul yes/no probability, Choice over up to 255 options, Score). The open ecosystem formed within days and now has its own category here (**Decision layer**): [laya](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya) (31k★ in three weeks), [kev](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev) (self-trainable Jev-like models on Qwen), [AnyJev](https://github.com/nokia-applied-research/AnyJev) (Nokia Applied Research; any LLM → decision model, Apache-2.0), [SemIf-OpenJev](https://github.com/TheoLeeCJ/SemIf-OpenJev) (runs on a single 3090). RAG-specific tooling is forming on the [Radar](../README.md#radar). Of the seven lists we audit, one (Danielskry/Awesome-RAG) already links Jev and AnyJev; the open engines appear in none of them. Explainer: [What is Jev (Firecrawl)](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/what-is-jev).

Caveat, as always: the benchmark numbers circulating in writeups (nDCG gains, per-query costs) are vendor- and community-reported; treat them as leads, not facts, until reproduced independently.
